## Rulings (controller, 2026-11-14; recorded in `.superpowers/sdd/plan-c/ledger.md`)

- **R-A1 held names:** the veto **blocks entry only**. A held name is never sold because of it (a forced
  exit is uncapped and would churn). So the veto is not an `EligibilityReason` (that would force-sell via
  `EXIT_ELIGIBILITY_LOST` and move the liquidity/loser-decile populations); it is applied in
  `decide_book` by adding the vetoed codes to `blocked`, after the screen.
- **R-A2 the rule is the existing deterministic agent's rule:** `MarketAgent`'s weights
  (limit_up 0.65, consecutive_board 0.85, broken_board −0.65, threshold −0.15). A name is vetoed on
  session t when it has at least one limit event in the 5 sessions ending at t and the mean weight of
  those events is < −0.15 ("bearish"). Rule id `market_bearish_5/v1`. The weights move to one shared
  constant in `domain/limit_events.py` and `MarketAgent` reads it (behavior unchanged).
- **R-B1 two factors, family `limit_events`:** `limit_up_count_20` (sessions in the last 20 that closed at
  the up limit) and `broken_board_count_20` (sessions in the last 20 that touched the up limit intraday and
  did not close there). Computed from `daily` + `stk_limit` already in the panel; no new dataset. Declared
  direction `lower_is_better` for both (A-share short-horizon overreaction); the reading tests the
  declared direction one-sided -- no flipping after the read.
- **R-R1 the read:** window `2024-01-02..2026-09-24` (the only unread historical window; one read for A and
  B together), stride 20 sessions, horizon h20, the G-0 engine's eligible cross-section. Three tests,
  Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.10 across all three:
  1. A: per sampled session, mean h20 return of vetoed survivors minus mean h20 return of all survivors;
     candidate if the mean of that difference is < 0 with one-sided p after BH ≤ 0.10 **and** at least
     60 vetoed survivor observations in total; fewer than 60 → `not_measurable`.
  2. B: h20 rank IC of `limit_up_count_20` (oriented by its declared direction).
  3. B: h20 rank IC of `broken_board_count_20` (oriented).
  B's candidate rule: mean oriented rank IC > 0 with BH-adjusted one-sided p ≤ 0.10. Outcomes:
  `candidate` / `no_evidence` / `not_measurable` per test. The thresholds are frozen in the claim file
  before the read; the reader refuses a second read.
