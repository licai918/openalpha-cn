`update_panel(runtime_dir, session=, as_of=, exchange=adapter.exchange, targets=options.targets or targets_for_survivor(builds), next_year=next_year_calendar_needed(calendar, session=session, horizon=20), survivor=SurvivorPanelUpdate(max_catch_up_sessions=adapter.max_panel_catch_up_sessions))`; `build_factors(...)` builds the session and up to 20 missed instants (catch-up needs them: `build_facts_day` reads only the build at the session's own 16:30 and refuses with `MissingFactorBuildError` otherwise). 5 paper head: `ordinals = SessionOrdinals(calendar, adapter.epoch_session)`; `index = ordinals.index_of(session)`; empty store -> `open_book(store=, session=epoch, session_index=0, cash=book.capital, config_digest=digest)` (+ the write-once marker); `session == epoch` -> only open, no evening; head = newest snapshot; `held` = its position codes, PINNED in the journal. 6 facts: `build_facts_day(store=panel, session=, as_of=<journal as_of>, config=book, factor_ids=book.composite_factors, transform=FACTOR_TRANSFORMS.get(adapter.transform), exchange=adapter.exchange, min_coverage=adapter.min_coverage, max_carry_sessions=adapter.max_carry_sessions, held=held)` then `screen(facts.facts, book)`; held-status refusals are NOT applied here (they belong to the paper book; the prediction must not be lost to them). 7 prediction: filed BEFORE the paper step so a paper-book data fault never costs the forward record (the only evidence). 8 paper book: (a) catch-up: for each k in `missing_sessions(store, config_digest=digest, through_index=index-1)` in order: `day = ordinals.day_of(k)`; if the head has positions: read facts for `day` at the run's `as_of` with `min_coverage=0.0` and `held=positions(head)`, apply the held refusals, take `delisted`; else no facts read and `delisted = ∅`; (b) re-read facts if `held` changed after catch-up (eligible/survivor sets do not depend on `held`; only the rows of long-halted holdings do). (c) held statuses from `FactsDay.held_status`: REFUSE the day by name with a remedy: `no_bar_in_reach`, `not_in_registry`, `not_yet_listed`, `board_unrecognised`; PASS ON `delisted` into the `delisted` argument; `row` normal. (d) `market = settlement_market(..., names=held' ∪ pending names)`. (e) `run_evening(store, ledger, make_portfolio, config=book, session=, session_index=index, market=, screen=, entrant_prices={f.ts_code: f.close for f in facts.facts if f.ts_code not in held'}, delisted=, observed_on=)`, `make_portfolio = lambda ledger: make_paper_portfolio(ledger, adapter.paper_limits)`. (f) `observed_on` = the journal-pinned `as_of`'s Shanghai date (deterministic reruns; `settle_session`/`advance` refuse `session > observed_on`). 9 summary. When today's facts are refused (`FactsCoverageError`, `MissingFactorBuildError`, `StaleRegistryError`): the run stops at step 6 with exit 1, no prediction, no paper step; the next run's step 8a settles that session with no decision. NEVER settle automatically on a refusal (a same-evening rerun after a fix would then find the session settled and could never decide it). A persistent held-status refusal also blocks the catch-up of that session: the book stalls until the data is fixed; the stall is named. After 09:15 of the next session: settle only (decision 2).
