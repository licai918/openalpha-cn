# V2-P7-018 Part E — the survivor forward report and the late-record check: design

> Durable condensed copy (2026-10-14) of the Part E design memo (read-only research at integration-branch tip c25a3c1, which holds D5b). The plan `2026-10-14-p7-step0-adapters-and-ledger.md` (Part E) points here. The protocol decisions in section 6 are RECOMMENDATIONS for the V2-P7-006 checkpoint; they are NOT adopted until the user confirms them there. Part E's code reads the protocol from the registration's settings and refuses when it is absent.

## 1. What `scripts/forward_report.py` does today and where the survivor seam goes

Inputs: `--runtime-dir`, `--registration`, `--repo`, `--as-of`, `--json`; the panel store and the prediction store; from the runtime directory the P6 journal (`daily_selection/<config_id[:16]>/`), provenance (`daily_selection_provenance/`) and verdicts (`reports/record_verdicts/`). Flow: `_admit` (`admit_registration(..., also_bound=("scripts/forward_report.py",))`, so the report script is itself bound by the registration's code binding) -> `forward_rebalances` (store-witnessed schedule via `witnessed_days`) -> `forward_record_check` (`RecordCheck` with provenance lookup and `FileVerdicts`) -> a `prediction_ids` request -> `backtest_strategy(..., verify_late=check)` -> `forward_summary` (sections: `unverifiable_inputs_corrected_after_filing`; `statistics.{all_periods, excluding_unverifiable}`; `significance` = the holdout's one-sided sign-flip test of the non-overlapping period net excess over the registered benchmark; `unprovable_holds`; `integrity`). No verdict wording: numbers and p-values labelled "headline: the candidate book". A late record the check REFUSES raises `StrategyBacktestError` -> `ForwardReportError`: the whole P6 report is refused; UNVERIFIABLE records are priced, flagged, and get a sensitivity run. The verdict cache keeps verified verdicts only, write-once, named `<record>.<provenance>.<digest of partitions>.json`. Exits: 0 written to `RT/reports/forward-<date>.json`; 1 on `ForwardReportError`, `HoldoutRefusedError`, `StrategyViewError`, `StepFailedError`; argparse 2.
A survivor registration today is refused NOT through `strategy_arguments` but through `_registered_anchor` (`KeyError('start')` -> "names no start date" -> exit 1): a misleading message with no test. The seam: in `forward_report()` right after `_admit`: `survivor = _survivor_of(admitted)` (`daily_selection.survivor_registration`, lazy import); if not None: `return _survivor_forward_report(...)` returning a `SurvivorForwardReport` (frozen dataclass: `as_of`, `config_id`, `document`); `forward_report_view` and `summary_lines` branch on it; the survivor output file is `forward-survivor-<date>.json` (no collision with P6's name); the P6 except tuple is unchanged (survivor errors subclass `ForwardReportError`); every survivor module is imported lazily (`backtest.paper` installs an audit hook); the existing forward-report test file must pass UNMODIFIED, run before and after, plus a fresh-interpreter `sys.modules` test.

## 2. The survivor report's content

All records are `survivor_book_composite/v1`, horizon 20d; scope = eligible names, score = composite.
**(a) Prediction layer (the only evidence layer).** Witnessed records: bound (`survivor_record_is_bound`), registered before the cutoff of its session, session ordinal >= 1, EXACTLY one bound record per session else the report is refused; the store is the witness (a journal's `prediction` stage must name the store's record id; a day refused at step 8 has a record and no `result`). Labels from the shared reader (`model_view.OutcomeLabels`): entry = close of D+1, exit = close of D+1+h (h=20: `outcome_known_at`; h=5 reads the SAME record with a 5-session window); a record is mature only when the report's `as_of` is at or after the 16:30 of the exit day; immature records are COUNTED, never labelled; unlabelled names counted per decile and per refusal code (`halted_session`, `delisted_in_window`, `locked_at_limit`, ...). Deciles over the whole scope BEFORE outcomes, worst first by `(score, ts_code)` as the kernel; survivors reconstruct `screen().survivors`. Rank IC = tie-aware Spearman (`factor_ic.average_ranks` + Pearson); `None` with a code when a side is degenerate. Reports: investable equal-weight (mean label over the labelled scope, gross buy-and-hold), `survivors_minus_ew`, `ew_minus_worst_decile` (EW less the worst decile; every tested metric is oriented so that greater than 0 supports the screen), `top_minus_bottom`, CSI 500 (`000905.SH` close ratio over the same closes; context only). Effective sample: NON-OVERLAPPING sampling (sessions `anchor + h*k` on CALENDAR sessions; a sampled session with no or an excluded record is a GAP and does not shift later picks), the test is `outcome_statistics.sign_flip_test` (the repository's own `grid.non_overlapping` rule), not Newey-West (T8's t and the power table are in non-overlapping 20-day periods; NW with lag 19 on <250 overlapping observations is biased and adds an unregistered bandwidth); the mean over all daily records may be printed as a descriptive point estimate with NO standard error. Before the pre-registered window is satisfied: counts (witnessed, mature, sampled, gaps, unlabelled by code) and descriptive means labelled "descriptive, not a test"; NO p-value and NO verdict word; "not tested: k of K pre-registered periods matured".
**(b) Paper ledger: a record layer, never tested.** Equity curve, N held, cash share, gross exposure from SNAPSHOTS (never `runner_record`); one-sided turnover = sum of filled `execution.notional` (write-offs excluded) / (2 x mean equity), annualised at 244; costs by component (commission, `transfer_fee` = slippage, stamp duty) cross-checked against the change in `book.fees_paid`; write-offs (`reason == "delisting_write_off"`, `execution is None`; cost basis = -`realized_pnl_delta`; share of equity; the D16 sensitivity "recovered at the last mark" = ledger quantity x the previous snapshot's mark, in LEDGER units, not a real close); attempts filled/rejected by reason from the ledger, but `not_attempted`, expired and cancelled orders exist only in the journals; unfunded/unaffordable/frozen/near-exit/deferred exits/exits by reason from evening decisions only (catch-up sessions have no decision); illiquid exits; coverage of the record (evenings, settle-only days, caught-up sessions, refused days).
**(c) Relative return.** The window chain on the sampled sessions: book return `equity(exit)/equity(entry)-1` from snapshots vs that record's investable EW and CSI 500 over the same closes, compounded, gaps break the chain and are named. The benchmark close is NOT persisted by the paper book: read `index_daily` at report time. Label: the book is net of the protocol's costs and cash; the benchmarks are gross buy-and-hold; the paper book fills at the D+1 open while labels start at the D+1 close (a one-leg timing difference stated).
**(d) UNVERIFIABLE share** (D2 rule 3): UNVERIFIABLE/checked and refused/checked, overall and within the sample; headline = verified + UNVERIFIABLE (the P6 convention), sensitivity = UNVERIFIABLE excluded (their sampled sessions become gaps); each UNVERIFIABLE record listed with its changes. **(e) Late-record check results**: verified (of which cached), UNVERIFIABLE (`recompute_differs` / `recompute_failed`), refused with reason, not bound, no provenance; the `INTEGRITY` statement.
Wording: every section carries `SURVIVOR_WORDING`; the prediction layer adds "candidate forecast of a registered research configuration; not a performance claim"; no "effective", "confirmed" or "alpha". It must NOT claim: any return or comparison with the exploratory numbers (T8's +6.1-6.3% is optimistic by ~0.9 pp a year: do not print expected numbers next to forward results); any significance on the paper book (IR 0.5 needs ~16 years); anything from the spent window 2024-01-02..2026-09-24; any wording upgrade from a secondary metric, from h=5, or from a test passed before K.

## 3. The survivor late-record check (`SurvivorRecordCheck.__call__(record)`)
1 bound first (`survivor_record_is_bound`; not bound -> refused, never recomputed). 2 provenance by `(session, batch_digest(record.batch))` from `RT/daily_survivor_provenance/*.json`: the index SKIPS other registrations' files BEFORE `read_survivor_provenance` (P6's `continue` pattern; calling it on every file would refuse the report for a foreign file); two files naming one key refuse the report; no provenance -> refused. 3 cache: a kept verdict whose key equals the current key -> verified (`cached=True`). 4 recompute: `recompute_survivor_batch(..., provenance=provenance)`; `differs = (again.as_of, again.artifact, again.predictions) != (batch.as_of, batch.artifact, batch.predictions)`; `except SURVIVOR_RECOMPUTE_REFUSALS` -> scope `recompute_failed`; `SurvivorCheckArgumentError`, `TypeError`, `PanelCatalogBusyError` PROPAGATE. 5 equal -> verified, `verdicts.keep`. 6 otherwise `survivor_provenance_changes(store, provenance, transform=, scope=)`: non-empty -> UNVERIFIABLE; empty -> refused (value-blind in-place corrections end here by design).
The cache is keyed by CONTENT STATE, never by census. Finding: content hashes move on every append and `stock_basic` is restamped for every lifecycle year on each fetch, so a pure content-hash key NEVER hits while the daily run continues. Recommended (decision 13): key the three small through-session datasets (`stock_basic`, `namechange`, `trade_cal`) by a windowed value digest (`panel_ingest.stored_rows_digest(..., through=session, visible_at=16:30)`, P6's form) and the rest by content hash: the cache then hits for every record whose year has closed and still misses on any in-place correction. Files write-once in `RT/reports/survivor_record_verdicts/`, apart from P6's; an unreadable file is a miss with a warning. Cost per record: one recompute = one facts day ~2.1 s at 2026 scale (up to x2 on the not-yet-knowable fallback, +~10 s for a record holding a name halted ~250 sessions); a cache-key check ~0.6-1.2 s; for a 250-record window ~9 min weekly without a cache (~18 min worst case), labels +1-2 min. NO sampling policy: every record that enters a statistic must be checked (an unchecked record is not evidence); check every bound record, mature or not, so the UNVERIFIABLE share is over all records.

E3: equity from snapshots not `runner_record`; write-off by reason with `execution is None`; D16 "last mark" from the snapshot n-1; turnover excludes write-offs; costs by component equal the `fees_paid` change; `not_attempted` counted from journals only; catch-up sessions excluded from decision counts; journal vs store snapshot-id mismatch refused; window chain and gap break. E4: P6 forward-report tests unmodified before and after; fresh-interpreter `sys.modules` test; survivor end to end on D5's scripted-market fixtures; exit 1 on missing protocol settings, an argument error or duplicate records; wording in every section; no verdict before K.

## 6. What cannot be evaluated yet, and the protocol decisions (V2-P7-006; recommendations, NOT adopted)
State in every report until it no longer holds: no survivor record exists yet (daily registration paused since 2026-10-08; the configuration is registered once at V2-P7-006 after D and E); the first h=20 outcome matures ~21 sessions after the first evening; the window needs K periods; G-0 needs conclusions on the research and validation windows first; 2024-01-02..2026-09-24 is spent; paper equity is not evidence; T8's expected numbers are ~0.9 pp a year optimistic (T9) and must be re-measured; 2021's IC was +0.037; the h=5 IC dispersion has never been measured; no null-signal control yet (V2-P7-002).
Decisions (power uses a between-period standard deviation of 0.15): 1 primary metric = mean RANK IC at h=20 on the non-overlapping sample; 2 test = one-sided sign-flip (`outcome_statistics.sign_flip_test`), 100000 samples, a new seed written into the registration, one-sided p <= 0.05, family size 1; 3 window K = 36 non-overlapping 20-day periods (~3 years) from the anchor, a single analysis at K (power at IC 0.05 ~60%, at 0.10 ~99%; K=12 gives ~25% at 0.05); 4 before K: counts and descriptive means only, no interim test (alternative: an interim p-value at 12 periods labelled "interim, not the test"); 5 multiple testing: secondary family {h20 survivors-EW, h20 EW minus worst decile (`ew_minus_worst_decile`), h5 rank IC} under BY with `arbitrary` dependence, q=0.10; secondaries never upgrade wording; 6 anchor = ordinal 1 (the first evening), pinned in the protocol; 7 unlabelled names excluded from IC and means, unlabelled share per decile reported; 8 UNVERIFIABLE: headline includes, sensitivity excludes; a conclusion only if both agree in sign; 9 refused records: excluded and listed, the report is still produced (exit 0); if any refused record falls in the sample at K the test is printed "compromised (n refused)"; 10 paper book: descriptive only; 11 benchmarks: investable EW = the record's scope, gross, buy-and-hold over the label window, and CSI 500, both reported; 12 the protocol lives in `settings["survivor_forward_protocol"]` (versioned, `extra="forbid"`; the config's top-level keys are closed); 13 verdict cache: content hash plus windowed value digests for `stock_basic`, `namechange`, `trade_cal`; 14 label `as_of` = the report's `as_of`, printed as `label_as_of`.
